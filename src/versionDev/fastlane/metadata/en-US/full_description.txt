DataDrive keeps your files safe in the cloud and in sync across your devices.

Features:
* Easy, modern interface
* Upload files to DataDrive
* Share them with others
* Keep your favorite files and folders synced
* Search across all folders
* Auto Upload for photos and videos taken by your device
* Keep up to date with notifications
* Multi-account support
* Secure access to your data with fingerprint or PIN

Learn more at https://datadrive.vn

DataDrive is based on the open source Nextcloud Android app (GPLv2).
